# Country flag assets

The `4x3` directory contains local country flag SVGs used by the country autocompletes.

- Most flags are from [flag-icons 7.5.0](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), licensed under the MIT License. The license text is stored in `flag-icons-LICENSE`.
- `ac.svg` is the [Flag of Ascension Island](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Ascension_Island.svg) from Wikimedia Commons and is marked public domain.
- `ta.svg` is the [Flag of Tristan da Cunha](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Tristan_da_Cunha.svg) from Wikimedia Commons and is marked public domain.

The file names use lowercase ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 or libphonenumber region codes.
